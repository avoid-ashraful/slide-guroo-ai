## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Navigate to the **Sign Up** page
2. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Full Name**: Your complete name
   - **Username**: Choose a unique username (3-20 characters)
   - **Email**: Your valid email address
   - **Password**: Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters)
   - **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password
3. Click **Sign Up**
4. Check your email for verification link
5. Click the verification link in your email

**Note**: You can login before verifying your email, but some features require verification.

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the **Login** page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Sign In**

### First Time Setup

After logging in:
1. Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox
2. You'll see a banner if your email isn't verified
3. Once verified, you can access all features

## Account Management

### Email Verification

**Why verify?** Email verification is required to:
- Upload slides and create lessons
- Generate topic-based lessons
- Ask questions in the Q&A system

**How to verify:**
1. Check your email inbox for the verification link
2. Click the link in the email
3. You'll be redirected to a success page

**Didn't receive the email?**
1. Go to `/verify-email` page
2. Enter your email address
3. Click **Send Verification Email**

### Resetting Your Password

**If you forgot your password:**
1. Click **Forgot your password?** on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for reset link
4. Click the link (valid for 1 hour)
5. Enter your new password
6. Click **Reset Password**
7. Login with your new password

### Viewing Your Profile

Click on your avatar in the top-right corner to see:
- Your full name
- Email address
- Verification status
- Quick links to Dashboard

## Creating Lessons

SlideGuroo offers two ways to create AI-powered lessons:

### Method 1: Upload Slides

Perfect for when you have existing presentations.

**Steps:**
1. Click **Upload Slides** in the navigation
2. Choose your settings:
   - **Language**: English or বাংলা (Bangla)
   - **Difficulty Level**: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced
   - **Include Diagrams**: Toggle on/off
3. Click **Choose File** and select your presentation
   - Supported formats: PPT, PPTX, PDF
   - Maximum size: 50 MB
4. Click **Generate Lesson**
5. Wait while AI processes your slides (usually 30-60 seconds)
6. View your generated lesson!

**Supported File Types:**
- PowerPoint: `.ppt`, `.pptx`
- PDF: `.pdf`

**Tips for best results:**
- Use slides with clear, concise content
- Include diagrams and images
- Ensure text is readable
- Avoid overly complex slides

### Method 2: Generate from Topic

Perfect for learning new subjects from scratch.

**Steps:**
1. Click **Learn Topic** in the navigation
2. Enter your topic:
   - Example: "Photosynthesis"
   - Example: "Introduction to Python Programming"
   - Example: "World War II"
3. Choose your settings:
   - **Language**: English or বাংলা
   - **Difficulty Level**: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced
   - **Include Diagrams**: Toggle on/off
   - **Context** (optional): Add specific focus
     - Example: "For Class 10 students"
     - Example: "Focus on practical applications"
4. Click **Generate Lesson**
5. Wait while AI creates your personalized lesson
6. View your generated lesson!

**Topic Ideas:**
- Science: "Cell Structure", "Newton's Laws", "Chemical Bonding"
- Math: "Quadratic Equations", "Calculus Basics", "Probability"
- History: "Renaissance Period", "Ancient Egypt", "Industrial Revolution"
- Programming: "Python Basics", "Web Development", "Data Structures"
- Languages: "English Grammar", "Spanish Vocabulary", "Bengali Literature"

## Viewing and Learning

Each lesson contains:

1. **Title and Summary**
   - Overview of the topic
   - Key learning objectives

2. **Sections**
   - Organized by subtopics
   - Clear, progressive content
   - Adapted to your difficulty level

3. **Key Points**
   - Important concepts highlighted
   - Easy-to-remember takeaways

4. **Examples**
   - Real-world applications
   - Practical scenarios
   - Relatable analogies

5. **Diagrams** (if enabled)
   - Visual representations
   - Interactive flowcharts
   - Process diagrams
   - Concept maps

6. **Prerequisites**
   - What you should know before starting
   - Related topics to review

7. **Next Topics**
   - Suggested topics to continue learning
   - Progressive learning path

### Reading a Lesson

**Navigation:**
- Scroll through sections sequentially
- Each section builds on previous knowledge
- Take your time to understand each concept

**Diagrams:**
- Diagrams are interactive and can be zoomed
- Hover over elements for more details
- Download diagrams if needed

**Taking Notes:**
- Use the Q&A feature to ask clarifying questions
- Save important questions and answers
- Review chat history later

## Interactive Q&A

The Q&A system allows you to ask questions about any lesson.

### Asking Questions

1. Scroll to the Q&A section at the bottom of the lesson
2. Type your question in the input box
3. Choose your language (English or বাংলা)
4. Click **Ask Question**
5. Wait for AI response (usually 5-10 seconds)

### Types of Questions You Can Ask

**Clarification Questions:**
- "What does [term] mean?"
- "Can you explain [concept] in simpler terms?"
- "I don't understand [section], can you help?"

**Example Requests:**
- "Can you give me more examples of [concept]?"
- "Show me a real-world application"
- "Provide an analogy for [topic]"

**Deeper Understanding:**
- "Why is [concept] important?"
- "How does [A] relate to [B]?"
- "What are the implications of [concept]?"

**Application Questions:**
- "How would I use this in practice?"
- "Can you give me a problem to solve?"
- "What are common mistakes to avoid?"

### Understanding Responses

Each response includes:

**Main Answer:**
- Detailed explanation
- Adapted to your level
- In your chosen language

**Related Concepts:**
- Connected topics
- Links to other parts of the lesson
- Further reading suggestions

**Additional Examples:**
- More practical examples
- Different perspectives
- Analogies and metaphors

**Follow-up Questions:**
- Suggested next questions
- Help guide your learning
- Deepen your understanding

### Conversation History

- All your questions and answers are saved
- Access them later from your Dashboard
- Review difficult concepts
- Track your learning progress

## Dashboard

Your personal learning hub.

### Accessing the Dashboard

Click **Dashboard** in the navigation or your avatar dropdown.

### Statistics Overview

View your learning metrics:

**Total Lessons**: Number of lessons you've created
**From Uploads**: Lessons from your presentations
**From Topics**: Lessons generated from topics
**Questions Asked**: Total Q&A interactions

### Lessons List

See all your lessons in one place:

**Features:**
- **Filter**: By upload or topic source
- **Sort**: By date created
- **View**: Click to open lesson
- **Delete**: Remove lessons you no longer need

**Each lesson shows:**
- Title
- Source type (Upload or Topic)
- Number of sections
- Creation date

### Recent Activity

Track your learning journey:
- Recent questions asked
- Latest lessons created
- Chat history snippets
- Timestamps for everything

### Managing Lessons

**Deleting a Lesson:**
1. Find the lesson in your dashboard
2. Click **Delete** button
3. Confirm deletion
4. Lesson and all chat history will be removed

**Warning**: Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone!

## Tips and Best Practices

### Getting the Best Lessons

**For Slide Uploads:**
- Use well-structured presentations
- Include visual elements
- Keep slides concise
- One topic per slide
- Use bullet points

**For Topic Generation:**
- Be specific with your topic
- Add context for better results
- Specify your education level
- Mention what you want to focus on

### Learning Effectively

**Before Starting:**
- Check prerequisites
- Set aside focused time
- Have a notebook ready
- Eliminate distractions

**While Learning:**
- Read sections sequentially
- Don't rush through content
- Ask questions when confused
- Review diagrams carefully
- Take notes on key points

**After Completing:**
- Review next topics
- Ask follow-up questions
- Practice with examples
- Revisit difficult sections

### Using Q&A Effectively

**Do:**
- Ask specific questions
- Request examples
- Ask for clarification
- Follow up on confusing points
- Use simple language

**Don't:**
- Ask multiple questions at once
- Expect answers outside lesson scope
- Skip reading the lesson first
- Use overly complex language

### Language Selection

**English:**
- More technical resources available
- Precise terminology
- International standards

**বাংলা (Bangla):**
- Better for native speakers
- Easier to understand
- Culturally relevant examples
- Local context

**Tip**: You can generate a lesson in one language and ask questions in another!

### Difficulty Levels

**Beginner:**
- Simple explanations
- Basic concepts
- More examples
- Less technical jargon

**Intermediate:**
- Balanced approach
- Standard terminology
- Practical applications
- Some advanced concepts

**Advanced:**
- In-depth coverage
- Technical details
- Complex relationships
- Professional level

## Getting Help

**Common Issues:**

**Can't login?**
- Check email and password
- Try password reset
- Clear browser cache

**Email verification not working?**
- Check spam folder
- Request new verification email
- Wait a few minutes

**Lesson generation failed?**
- Check file size (max 50 MB)
- Try a different file format
- Ensure stable internet
- Try again later

**Questions not working?**
- Verify email first
- Check internet connection
- Ensure lesson is loaded
- Try refreshing page
